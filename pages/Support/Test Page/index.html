Why your support matters

We are Nashley and Nadia Adams, identical twin sisters who were diagnosed with scoliosis ourselves. We started this non-profit to support patients and spread more awareness, so no one feels alone.

Awareness

Helps us keep raising awareness of scoliosis, including during National Scoliosis Awareness month in June.

Patient support

Helps us make and hand out “gifts of hope” bags, so patients know they are not alone.

Medical costs

Goes toward the fundraising needed for patients’ emergency medical procedures.
