Frequently asked questions

How can I contact the foundation?

You can use the form on this page, email nascoli.foundation@gmail.com, or message us on WhatsApp on 068 142 7846.

Can I share my scoliosis journey?

Yes, we welcome personal stories. Choose "Sharing personal experiences" in the form, or reach out by email or WhatsApp and tell us a little about yourself.

How can I get involved?

If you would like to volunteer or explore a partnership, choose "Volunteering" or "Partnerships" in the form and tell us what you have in mind.

How can I support the foundation?

To ask about donating or other ways to help, choose "Donations" in the form or contact us by email or WhatsApp, and we will point you in the right direction.

Your story matters.

Whether you need support, want to share your experience or simply have a question, we would love to hear from you.
