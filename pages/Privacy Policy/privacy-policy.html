Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Entity: NA Scoliosis Foundation NPC (Reg. No. 2021/660834/08 | NPO No. 265-653) | Jurisdiction: Republic of South Africa (POPIA)

NA Scoliosis Foundation NPC ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and dignity of our scoliosis community, donors, volunteers, patients, families, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information in compliance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Personal Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifying information when you browse our website unless you voluntarily provide it. Depending on how you engage with us, we may process:

  • Contact, Story & Volunteer Inquiries: When you contact us via our website forms, email (nascoli.foundation@gmail.com), or official WhatsApp line (068 142 7846), we collect your name, email address, phone number, and any feedback, encouragement, or personal story you choose to share.
  • Health & Special Personal Information: Because our mission supports individuals living with scoliosis (including initiatives such as our "Gifts of Hope" care bags and awareness outreach), you may voluntarily share health-related details in your messages or volunteer applications. Under POPIA Section 26, health information is classified as Special Personal Information and is processed strictly with your explicit consent.
  • Donations & Financial Contributions: When you donate via our Support page, we may receive donor details (name, email, donation amount, and reference) for acknowledgments and non-profit financial records. Online card and fundraising payments are processed externally via Yoco and GivenGain, and direct EFT donations are processed via First National Bank (FNB). We do not store credit or debit card numbers on our website servers.
  • Technical, Cookie & Usage Data: We use basic cookies and aggregated analytics to measure visitor traffic across sections of the site and maintain website accessibility and performance.

2. How and Why We Use Your Information

We process personal information lawfully and strictly for our non-profit objectives:

  • Responding to support messages, personal stories, community ideas, and general inquiries.
  • Coordinating volunteer participation and distributing "Gifts of Hope" support packages.
  • Processing and acknowledging donations, maintaining statutory accounting records, and complying with South African Revenue Service (SARS) and NPO Directorate auditing requirements.
  • Improving website accessibility, content relevance, and technical security.

3. Consent & Information Relating to Children

Scoliosis frequently affects children and adolescents. In accordance with POPIA Section 34, NA Scoliosis Foundation does not knowingly collect personal or health information directly from anyone under the age of 18 without the verified consent of a parent or legal guardian. If a minor contacts us, their contact details are used solely to respond to the immediate inquiry and are not retained in a retrievable database or used for follow-up outreach without parental consent.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & External Links

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or health information for marketing purposes. We only share limited data with trusted third parties when strictly necessary to operate our foundation or comply with South African law:

  • Payment & Fundraising Platforms: Yoco, GivenGain, and First National Bank (FNB) for processing donations securely under their respective privacy and PCI-DSS security standards.
  • Communication & Hosting Tools: Website hosting infrastructure, email services, and WhatsApp Business when you choose to message us on those channels.
  • Statutory & Legal Obligations: Independent auditors, accountants, SARS, or legal advisors where required by law.

Our website also links to external awareness resources and partner organizations (such as Setting Scoliosis Straight and Higgy Bears). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

5. Cookies & Website Analytics

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to ensure smooth page navigation and help us understand aggregate visitor traffic. You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings without losing access to our educational content.

6. Data Security & Retention

We protect the confidentiality and integrity of your personal information through encrypted HTTPS connections, restricted administrative access, and data minimization. Inquiry records are kept only for as long as needed to assist you, while financial donation records are retained for the mandatory statutory period required under South African non-profit and tax legislation before being securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject in South Africa, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you and obtain a copy of it.
  • Correction or Deletion: Ask us to update inaccurate details or delete personal or health information we no longer have a lawful reason to retain.
  • Withdraw Consent & Opt Out: Withdraw consent or opt out of future foundation updates at any time by emailing nascoli.foundation@gmail.com.
  • Lodge a Complaint: Submit a complaint to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za if you believe your privacy rights have been infringed.
